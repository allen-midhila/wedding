# WED-02 Wedding Vision and Style

Index: [../INDEX.md](../INDEX.md)

- Wedding theme/style keywords (e.g., classic, modern, rustic, destination): Interfaith Kerala wedding blending Hindu and Christian traditions; cream/gold heritage palette; "lotus + cross" dual-tradition motif carried across stationery and decor
- Overall vibe (formal, semi-formal, casual): Traditional for the Engagement; festive for the Dubai Sangeet/Haldi; formal/ceremonial for the Mar Thoma church wedding
- Color palette: Cream and gold (Kerala traditional); kasavu off-white/gold tones
- Cultural/religious traditions to include: Hindu-style Engagement (Tirur), Dubai Sangeet/Haldi, and the Christian Mar Thoma Holy Matrimony (the wedding). No separate Hindu wedding ceremony.
- Must-have moments:
  - Christian Mar Thoma Holy Matrimony (24 Aug 2026, Thevalakkara) + parish-hall reception
  - Hindu-style Engagement (11 Jul 2026, Tirur)
  - Dubai Sangeet/Haldi celebration (~2 weeks before the wedding)
- Nice-to-have elements: Choreographed family performances and music at the Dubai Sangeet
- Top 3 priorities (ranked):
  1. Honoring both faiths equally and harmoniously in one coherent celebration
  2. Honouring the bride's Hindu side (Engagement) and the groom's Christian side (the Mar Thoma wedding)
  3. Staying within the ~₹20–25L self-funded budget

## Attire Direction (see WED-10)
- Engagement (Hindu style): traditional saree (bride), traditional wear (groom)
- Dubai Sangeet/Haldi: festive / Indo-western
- Christian wedding: wedding gown / formal (bride), suit (groom)
