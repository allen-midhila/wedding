Kerala–Dubai Hindu–Christian wedding master plan: events, budget, vendors, and logistics.

Kerala — Main ceremony days (Days 1–4)

Kerala · Day 1 Both families
Engagement & Nischayathartham / Ring Ceremony
Evening — Venue: wedding resort or ancestral home
Immediate family + close relatives (~100 guests). Exchange of rings, formal betrothal rituals from both Hindu and Christian traditions. Light dinner & music.
Kerala · Day 2 morning Hindu
Haldi / Nalungu
Morning — Venue: home/resort courtyard
Turmeric paste applied by family members to bride and groom at their respective homes. Separate ceremonies ideally, joined later. ~50 guests each. Flower decor, brass lamps.
Kerala · Day 2 evening Both
Sangeet / Musical Night
Evening — Banquet hall or resort lawn
Live Carnatic/folk music + choreographed dances from both families. MC, DJ, lighting. 200–300 guests. Kerala sadya-style snacks + cocktail spread. Photo booth setup.
Kerala · Day 3 morning Hindu
Hindu Wedding — Thiruvananthapuram style
Morning 8–11 AM — Mandap ceremony
Homam, kanyadanam, saptapadi, thali tying. Traditional Kerala music (Nadaswaram, Chenda). Full sadya lunch for all guests (~400). Kasavu saree, mundu veshti attire.
Kerala · Day 3 evening Christian
Christian Blessing / Solemnization
Evening 4–6 PM — Church or resort chapel setup
Blessing ceremony officiated by a pastor/priest. Exchange of vows, ring exchange, prayer. Western or fusion dress for bride. ~300 guests. Choir or live hymns. Followed by dinner.
Kerala · Day 4 Both families
Griha Pravesh / Home Welcome
Morning — Groom's family home or resort room
Bride welcomed into groom's home: stepping on rice, lamp lighting. Intimate family ritual (~30 people). Gift exchange, blessings from elders. Final Kerala farewell lunch.

Dubai — Grand reception (2–3 weeks later)

Dubai · Evening 1 Both families
Dubai Reception — Grand Celebration
Saturday evening — Banquet hall, 6 PM onwards
Cocktail hour 6–7 PM, grand entrance 7 PM. Multi-course dinner (Indian + continental menu). DJ + live singer. 300–400 guests including UAE-based family and friends. Formal couple portraits. First dance, family dances, open floor.
Dubai · Afternoon 2 Intimate
Dubai Family Brunch
Sunday afternoon — Hotel private dining
Relaxed brunch for close family (~60 people). Thank-you gifts for guests. Cake cutting. Casual dress. Serves as farewell for Kerala guests flying back.
Tip: Stagger Kerala and Dubai events by 2–3 weeks so the couple can rest and Kerala guests planning to attend Dubai have time to arrange travel and UAE visas.

Kerala — Recommended venue types

Heritage resort (best value)
Kumarakom Lake Resort, Uday Samudra (Kovalam), or CGH Earth properties. 5–6 acres, mandap space, banquet halls, in-house catering. Budget: ₹4–8L for full 3-day block booking.
Convention centre + home
Nair Service Society halls, Kerala Hotels halls, or TDM Hall (Trivandrum). More affordable but needs external catering. Budget: ₹1.5–3L for venue hire.

Guest accommodation — Kerala

  • Block-book 30–40 rooms at the main resort for outstation guests. Negotiate group rates (ask for 20–30% off rack rate for 3+ nights).
  • Shortlist 2–3 nearby hotels (3-star) for overflow guests: Hotel Residency Tower, Raj Park, or similar Trivandrum properties.
  • Arrange shuttle buses between overflow hotels and main venue every 90 minutes on event days.
  • Airport transfer service for VIP guests flying into TRV (Trivandrum International). Share cab-pool arrangements via WhatsApp group.

Dubai — Venue options

Ballroom hotel
JW Marriott Marquis, Sofitel Dubai, Grand Hyatt. Full-service, in-house décor and catering. Capacity 400–600. Budget: AED 80K–150K (₹18–35L).
Indian community halls
Kerala Samajam halls (Al Quoz, Oud Metha), IBPC Dubai. Budget-friendly, familiar to the community. Capacity 300–500. Budget: AED 15K–30K (₹3.5–7L).

Guest accommodation — Dubai

  • Kerala guests traveling to Dubai: block-book rooms at the wedding hotel or nearby property. Deira and Bur Dubai have affordable Indian-friendly hotels (AED 150–250/night).
  • UAE entry: Indian passport holders need a visa. Apply via Emirates/Air India (visa on arrival possible) or Dubai e-visa — at least 3–4 weeks in advance.
  • Arrange Careem/Uber corporate codes or a minibus for guest transfers on reception day.
Budget range: ₹20–50 Lakhs total. Breakdown below targets ₹38–40L mid-range. Savings are possible by choosing community halls over premium hotels for Dubai.

Invitation design

Traditional printed (primary)
Kerala-style box invitation in cream/gold with both traditions — lotus + cross motif. Bilingual: Malayalam + English. Inner cards: Hindu ceremony, Christian ceremony, Dubai reception.
Digital invite (WhatsApp)
Animated video invite (60–90 sec) with traditional music. Sent 6 weeks before Kerala, 4 weeks before Dubai. Includes Google Maps links for venues.

What to include in the invite

  • Event schedule card — all ceremonies with timings for Kerala days
  • Separate Dubai reception card (can be sent later as a second wave)
  • Hotel block info: resort name, contact, and booking code
  • RSVP link or WhatsApp number with deadline (3 weeks before)
  • Dress code guidance per event (kasavu for Hindu, semi-formal for Christian, formal for Dubai)
  • For Dubai guests: UAE visa application link and deadline reminder
  • Dietary preferences / allergy card for catering coordination

Send-out schedule

  • 3 months before Kerala: Save-the-date WhatsApp message for outstation and Dubai guests
  • 6 weeks before Kerala: Printed invites dispatched + digital video invite sent
  • 4 weeks before Kerala: RSVP deadline for Kerala events
  • 6 weeks before Dubai: Dubai-only invitation wave (for guests not attending Kerala)
  • 2 weeks before Dubai: Final headcount confirmation to Dubai venue and caterer
Recommended vendors: Invites by Lalitha (Trivandrum), WedMeGood digital invites, or local printing press in T'puram. Budget ₹30–60K for 500 printed sets.

Travel logistics — Kerala

  • Nearest airport: Trivandrum International (TRV). Fly-in guests from Dubai: IndiGo, Air India, Emirates all operate this route.
  • Train guests: Trivandrum Central is well connected. Pre-book Tatkal quota tickets for outstation family 2 months in advance.
  • Create a coordinated shuttle schedule: airport/station → hotel → venue for each event day. Share timetable in family WhatsApp group.
  • Create a shared Google Maps list with: main venue, overflow hotels, nearby hospitals, recommended restaurants for leisure days.

Travel logistics — Dubai

  • Visa for Indian nationals: Apply Dubai e-visa via Emirates, Air Arabia, or VFS Global. Processing: 3–5 working days. Cost: ~AED 250. Apply 4 weeks minimum before travel.
  • Main departure airports from Kerala: TRV (Trivandrum), COK (Kochi), or CCJ (Calicut). Emirates, IndiGo, Air India all fly direct to DXB.
  • Negotiate group airfare with Air India or IndiGo for a family block (10+ travelers) — possible 10–15% group discount.
  • Dubai venue deposit: typically 50% advance in AED. Maintain a Dubai AED float or transfer via Wise/bank for smooth payments.
  • Travel insurance for all Kerala guests flying to Dubai — especially elders. Covers medical emergencies and flight cancellations.

Day-of coordination

  • Assign one family coordinator per event (different person each day to avoid burnout)
  • Print a physical event order sheet for every vendor: times, sequence, contacts
  • Prepare welcome kits for outstation guests: itinerary, local SIM or WiFi info, venue map, emergency contacts
  • Set up a family WhatsApp broadcast list for real-time updates ("Gates open," "Sadya served")

Detailed milestone checklist

  • 12 months out: Fix wedding date (check auspicious muhurtham for Hindu ceremony), book Kerala venue, set budget split between Kerala and Dubai.
  • 9–10 months: Book photographer, videographer, Kerala wedding planner. Start attire shopping.
  • 6–8 months: Book Dubai venue, Kerala catering, decor vendor, music. Finalise guest list (Kerala vs Dubai split).
  • 4–5 months: Send save-the-dates. Design and print invitations. Confirm priest/pastor for both ceremonies. Book hotel room blocks.
  • 3 months: Send invitations. Finalise menus. Book shuttle vehicles. Order wedding cake (Dubai).
  • 2 months: Collect RSVPs. Begin UAE visa applications for Kerala guests. Book flights for family travel to Dubai.
  • 1 month: Final fittings. Confirm all vendors with day-of schedules. Create event order document. Pack outstation guest kits.
  • 1 week out: Final headcount to caterers. Distribute shuttles schedule. Brief all family coordinators.
  • Day before: Venue walkthrough with planner. Confirm photographer timings. Rest and relax!
Interfaith coordination tip: Meet with both the Hindu priest and Christian pastor together at least once (3 months out) to align on sequence, timings, and any shared ceremonial elements — this avoids surprises on the day.
